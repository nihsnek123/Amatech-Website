What is it?

- A computer cooling system consists of components that remove excess heat produced by the CPU, GPU, and other hardware. Common examples include air coolers, case fans, and liquid cooling systems.

Function

- The cooling system maintains safe operating temperatures, helps prevent overheating, and allows computer components to perform reliably during demanding tasks.

How it works with other components

- The cooling system removes heat generated by the CPU and GPU while they process data. Fans circulate air through the computer case, carrying hot air away from components. Some coolers connect to motherboard fan headers for power and speed control, helping maintain stable operating temperatures.
