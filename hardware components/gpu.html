What is it?

- The GPU is a specialized processor designed to handle graphics and perform many calculations simultaneously. It is commonly used for gaming, video editing, 3D rendering, and other graphics-intensive tasks.

Function

- The GPU renders images, videos, animations, and visual effects. It also accelerates certain computing tasks, including artificial intelligence and scientific calculations.

How it works with other components

- The GPU receives data and instructions from the CPU and uses its processing power to render graphics. It works with RAM and system storage to access necessary data. The motherboard provides its connection, while the power supply delivers electricity and the cooling system manages its temperature.
