What is it?

- The motherboard is the main circuit board of a computer. It connects the CPU, RAM, storage devices, GPU, and other components so they can communicate with one another.

Function

- The motherboard provides connections for computer components, allows them to exchange data, and distributes power to certain parts of the system.

How it works with other components

- The motherboard connects the CPU, RAM, GPU, and storage devices through sockets, slots, and data pathways. It allows these components to communicate while coordinating their connections with the power supply and other peripherals.
